# Brag Plan: WearID

## What is this app?
WearID is a universal clothing identity, sovereign biometric measurement passport, and deterministic body-to-garment sizing engine that ends sizing guesswork using pure millimeter ease modeling.

## The angle
"One Body. Four Different Sizes." Every clothing brand invents arbitrary vanity sizes (Zara L = H&M M = Westside S = Snitch XL). WearID decouples human anatomy from brand tags using client-owned biometric dimensions mapped against finished garment engineering specifications.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big editorial split screen: "ONE BODY. FOUR DIFFERENT SIZES." Rapid punch-in on brand tags flashing (Zara "L", H&M "M", Westside "S", Snitch "XL") followed by the sharp question: "Why is your size a guessing game?"

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Sovereign Passport (3s – 7s):** Clean dark editorial card reveals client-owned biometrics: Chest 1016mm, Waist 838mm, Inseam 812mm. Interactive 3D/SVG mannequin silhouette highlights anatomical landmark bands with pulsing ease indicators.
2. **Deterministic Drape & Ease Engine (7s – 12s):** Product match against verified apparel (Raymond, FabIndia, Snitch). Live 2D Drape Overlay reveals the body contour inside the finished garment with color-coded ease halos (Green = +60mm ideal torso ease, 96% fit confidence). 5-Axis Fit Radar locks in.
3. **Everywhere Portability (12s – 15s):** Instant WhatsApp tailor card generation and one-click sovereign retailer token checkout with zero raw biometrics exposed.

## Outro / punchline (15s – 18s)
"Your size, everywhere. Never guess a fit again." Bold WearID emblem lockup, forest green and warm ivory typography, and live domain: `wear-id.vercel.app`.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Sovereign passport with proportional body silhouette mannequin.
2. **Action:** Instant sizing match against verified brand cutting-table specs.
3. **Result:** 2D drape overlay ease halos + 96% confidence fit score.
