# Hyperframes Composition Brief: WearID

## Objective
Create a short, editorial fashion-tech launch video for WearID using Hyperframes.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 18.0 seconds (540 frames at 30fps)

## Source Material
- Project root: `e:/Projects/WearID`
- Primary files: `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/visual/BodySilhouetteCanvas.tsx`, `src/components/visual/GarmentDrapeOverlay.tsx`, `src/components/visual/FitRadarChart.tsx`
- Product name: WearID
- Tagline: "Your clothing identity, everywhere."
- Key visual moment: Proportional mannequin with pulsing landmark lines transitioning to 2D drape overlay with ease halos.

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Editorial fashion-tech film — quiet luxury, mathematical precision, typographic weight.
- Angle: Exposing the vanity sizing chaos across brands and unveiling deterministic millimeter fit modeling.
- Avoid: Generic tech SaaS animations, random stock vectors, illegible speed cuts.
